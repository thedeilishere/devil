About this app

ChallanCheck looks up traffic challans and vehicle registration details from a single number plate. Check pending fines, owner and RC information, and vehicle class in seconds — no government portal logins, no paperwork. Built for daily drivers, fleet owners, and anyone who wants a quick answer before a challan turns into a court notice.

What it does

Challan lookup

Enter a number plate to see pending and paid challans, fine amount, and issuing authority.

Vehicle & RC info

Pull registration class, fuel type, model, and registration date for any plate.

Search history

Every plate you've checked is saved locally, so repeat lookups are one tap away.

New challan alerts

Save a plate as a favorite and get notified the moment a new challan is recorded against it.

Data safety

No data shared with third parties

Number plates you search are stored only on your device.

Data is encrypted in transit

Requests to public vehicle records use a secure connection.

You can request data deletion

Clear your search history any time from Settings inside the app.
